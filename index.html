SurakshaNet is a mobile-first disaster preparedness and emergency response platform. Citizens get live disaster alerts, weather and earthquake awareness, one-tap SOS with emergency numbers, and the nearest shelters and hospitals. Authorities get a response dashboard to broadcast alerts and manage incidents from report to resolution.

What's inside

Live dashboard

Real-time alert feed, live map, weather warnings and earthquake updates for your area.

One-tap SOS

Share your location and dial 112, 108, 101 or 100 instantly, plus a family alert message.

Find help nearby

Nearest shelters, hospitals and relief camps with one-tap directions.

Authority response

Broadcast alerts, verify citizen reports and track incidents from reported to resolved.
